# Stats

Stats is a self-hosted analytics service for apostol.space projects. It keeps page views and named product events in a first-party system instead of relying on an external analytics platform.

## Reports

For each project, the dashboard shows:

- Traffic over a selected period
- Popular pages and product events
- Visitor countries

## Live activity

A live view shows events as they arrive, with filters for project, type, country, and other fields. A small script connects each site and follows navigation within single-page applications.

[Open the dashboard](https://stats.apostol.space).
