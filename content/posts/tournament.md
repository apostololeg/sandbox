# Tournament

Tournament runs small tournaments for table tennis, badminton, tennis, padel, squash, pool, darts, foosball, chess and more. Sign in with Google, put players into a pool, draw the matches, record scores, and follow the table.

## Two formats

- **Round robin:** everyone plays everyone, once or home and away. With an odd number of players, one rests each round
- **Knockout:** single elimination with standard seeding, so seeds 1 and 2 can only meet in the final. Missing spots become byes for the top seeds

## Player pool

The organiser types names or pastes a comma-separated list, then orders them for seeding. Anyone with the link can join while the pool is open, or press **That's me** on a typed-in name to link it to their account.

## Scores

- A match is best of 1, 3, 5 or 7, with presets for each sport
- Enter points per game, or just games won
- The organiser and the two players of a match can record it
- Knockout winners move on by themselves, and a result is locked once the next round has one

## Table and summary

A win is worth 3 points and a draw 1. Ties are split on head to head, then games, then points. The summary shows the champion, progress, biggest win, closest match, longest streak, most points and unbeaten players. The trophy icon opens a championship table across every tournament you play in.

## Notifications and app

Push notifications arrive when the draw is out, when your next match is ready, when someone records your result, when someone joins your pool, and when a champion is crowned. Tournament installs as an app. In the background, light pulses race through a draw sheet: at every junction one of each pair is knocked out, and the winner flares in the middle.
