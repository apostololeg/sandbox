# BestHumans

BestHumans is a public knowledge platform about people whose actions affect others. It organizes public figures into profiles and groups, with research focused on where their influence matters.

## Grounded public records

- Sourced impact and failure episodes instead of a single AI-generated score
- Profiles kept current from public information and news
- Community likes, daily voting, and one-to-one profile challenges
- Profile cards recorded on Base Sepolia

[Open BestHumans](https://besthumans.org).
