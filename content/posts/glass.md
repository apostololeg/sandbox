# GLASS

GLASS is a workspace for AI agents across typed chat, live voice, and phone calls. Agents can use connected tools and knowledge to answer questions and complete multi-step work.

## One workspace

- Create agents with their own instructions, voice, tools, and knowledge
- Explore places and routes on an interactive 3D map
- Attach files and photos, generate media, and organize chat storage
- Share conversations or fork them into another workspace
- Use the browser, mobile shell, or desktop wrapper

[Open GLASS](https://glass.cat).
