# GLASS

**GLASS is your own AI agency.** It is a workspace for AI agents across typed chat, live voice, and phone calls. You shape the agents yourself, and they use connected tools, knowledge, and their own cloud computer to answer questions and get multi-step work done.

![Paris landmarks an agent found, on the 3D map](/posts/glass/map.jpg)

## Agents you shape yourself

Every agent has its own **instructions, tools, knowledge, picture, and voice**. On an agent's page you write **Your rules** to shape how it works for you, and pick what it must **ask first** about or **never** do. Before an agent changes anything, a card shows exactly what will change. Mention an agent with **@** in the prompt to bring it into the conversation.

![An agent's page with its generated picture](/posts/glass/agent.jpg)

## 3D avatars

Open **Avatars** and describe a character to the **Avatar Creator**. A few minutes later you have a 3D model. Try its gestures, animations, and voice in the **Playground** tab, then give it to an agent.

![A generated avatar in the Playground](/posts/glass/avatar.jpg)

## Threads beside the chat

A thread opens in a **side panel** next to its chat, so you can follow both at once. Each side has its own message box, and the sidebar lists threads under their chat, grouped by date.

![A chat with its thread open in the side panel](/posts/glass/thread.jpg)

## Automations

Tell the **Automations manager** what to do and when, for example *"every weekday at 9:00, summarize my unread email"*. Each automation shows its schedule, a live countdown to the next run, and a log of every past run.

![A daily briefing automation with its schedule and instructions](/posts/glass/automations.jpg)

## Documents in Storage

Attach files and photos, generate media, and keep everything in **Storage**. Markdown files open as **live documents**: edit them as you read, changes are saved as you type, and you can watch an agent write into a document in real time.

![A briefing an agent wrote, open as a document](/posts/glass/document.jpg)

## Voice in your language

Talk to GLASS with **live voice**, or dictate and let the prompt **send itself** when you pause. Replies are read aloud in a voice that knows their language, and the text is highlighted as it is spoken.

## And more

- **3D map**: explore the places and routes your agents find, and fly along a route
- **Settings agent**: change any setting just by asking for it in chat
- **Claude Code**: connect it as an agent and chat with it from GLASS
- **Sharing**: share conversations or fork them into another workspace
- **Everywhere**: the browser, the iOS and Android apps, and the desktop app with local models
