# Music Legends

Music Legends is a group song game. Create a group, invite people, and play rounds built around a theme.

## How a round works

- Each person submits one song found with Spotify search
- Songs stay hidden until a point the creator chooses, then everyone can listen and vote
- Each person gets `floor(participants / 2)` votes, at most one per song
- When the round ends, a table shows every song and its vote count
- After a season ends, the group can open a table of rounds won and votes received
